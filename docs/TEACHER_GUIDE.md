# Teacher guide

1. **Sign in** with the account the administrator gave you and choose your own password.
2. **Classes → New class**, then paste your students (`Full name, email, student number`). Print or copy the temporary passwords: they are shown once. Students must change them at first sign-in. To reset one later: open the class and press *Reset password*.
3. **Question bank (optional):** write reusable questions. Maths goes between dollar signs, e.g. `$\frac{a}{b}$`; the toolbar inserts common notation. Add images with the camera button.
3b. **Students page:** search the students in your classes and export the list. To bring students in from a spreadsheet use the class page → *Import students from CSV…* (preview first; nothing is saved until you confirm).
4. **Exams → New exam:** set title, duration, window, attempts, shuffling and result release; tick the classes; add questions (new, or *From question bank*). Use *Save & check*; the portal lists any problems (no questions, missing options, total-mark mismatch, bad LaTeX...).
4b. **Students & Exam Enrollment** (on the exam page): click **+ Add Existing Students**, search, and press **Add** beside a student, or tick several and press *Add Selected Students*. You can also add a whole class, or import a roster CSV. Students you enroll this way do not need to be put in a new class. Enrolling does not start any timer; the exam's opening and closing times still apply. A student can be removed only until they have started.
5. **Preview as student**, then **Publish**. After publishing the questions are locked. To change them, *Duplicate* the exam.
6. **During the exam:** *Results & grading* shows who is in progress. Use *Extend time* (with a reason) or *Accommodation* for approved needs or technical problems.
7. **After:** multiple choice, true/false and numerical answers are marked automatically. Short answers that do not match exactly, and all written answers, wait for you under *Mark / review* (partial marks and feedback). Then *Finalize results* (blocked while anything is unmarked) and *Release*.
8. **Reports:** *Export Results CSV* opens in Excel (pending work is shown as pending, never as a score); the exam page also exports the roster. Class statistics only include fully-marked work.

## What the numerical marking rules mean
Correct value, tolerance (± absolute or % of value), optional rounding of the correct value to N decimals. Students may type `1/2`, `0.5` or `0,5`. Anything outside tolerance gets 0; answers that are not numbers wait for your review.

## Pass marks, terms and results
* In the exam editor, **Assessment details** holds the academic year, term, grade, **pass mark** (leave blank to use the school's default) and the **late grace** in seconds (how long after the timer ends a slow connection may still deliver its last answers). Opening and closing times are in the **school time zone** shown under the date boxes.
* A result shows *Pass* or *Not passed* only once all of its marking is finished. Until then you will see the marks so far labelled **provisional**. If results have already been released, the pass mark is locked so students never see a result change under them (withhold the results first if it truly must change).
* **Analytics** (top menu) summarises your published exams: use the filters, and read the grey box at the top, which says how many results are included and how many are still waiting to be marked. A question appears in "hardest questions" only after at least five graded answers.
* **Text size:** the A− / A+ buttons in the top bar enlarge text on that computer.
* **Printing:** use your browser's Print on the results page for a paper copy.
